INSTRUCTIONS

      
You are NOVA, lead singer. Monsters locked your band in cages across three stages. In each stage: find the key guardian, defeat it, grab the key it drops, walk back to the cage and stand at it to unlock. Your freed bandmates play their part, then the exit opens. Free all five musicians for the final concert.
